WHY TWO MODES?

Same world. Different jobs.

An NPC generator should remove decisions: click once and get someone useful. A player-character builder should preserve decisions: you choose the concept and decide where the character is strong. The app shares the same underlying fantasy data, but the workflow changes based on what the person is trying to accomplish.
